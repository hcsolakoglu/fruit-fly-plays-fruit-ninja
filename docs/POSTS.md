# Five X post concepts

## 1. Absurd / primary recommendation

I gave a fruit fly connectome a tiny sword.

4,386 neurons, real wiring, Fruit Ninja. Pixels go in; neural activity moves the blade. It slices fruit. It also hits bombs.

The sensory and motor interfaces are engineered. The mistakes are real.

**Pair with:** `06_fly_at_pc_3d.mp4` for the fly-at-PC presentation, or `01_tiny_sword.mp4` for the montage. Recommended primary: immediate, visual, and honest without requiring neuroscience context.

## 2. Technical

Pixels → visual neurons → measured fly synapses → descending activity → blade.

A reduced MaleCNS circuit playing Fruit Ninja, with a fitted readout and no pixel skip connection. Cut the wiring and it stops scoring.

**Pair with:** `02_pixels_to_neurons.mp4` or `03_cut_the_wiring.mp4`.

## 3. Scientific

Does real fly wiring make a better Fruit Ninja controller?

In this small experiment: no clear advantage over rewired connectivity. Both work; cutting the connections stops them.

A working closed loop, with a useful negative result.

**Pair with:** `05_real_vs_rewired.mp4`.

## 4. Minimal

A fruit fly connectome. A tiny sword. A few regrettable decisions.

Real synaptic wiring, simulated activity, engineered game controls.

**Pair with:** `04_fruit_vs_bomb.mp4`.

## 5. Result first

545 fruit sliced. 16 bombs hit.

A reduced fruit fly connectome played 20 seeded Fruit Ninja rounds. Blinded or disconnected, it sliced nothing. Rewired, it performed about the same.

The interesting part is what the controls tell us.

**Pair with:** `03_cut_the_wiring.mp4` or `05_real_vs_rewired.mp4`.
